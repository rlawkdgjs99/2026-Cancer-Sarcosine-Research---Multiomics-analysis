## Shared-axis wording

Recommended:

> A prespecified APC cross-presentation, TCR-signaling, TNFR2-related noncanonical NF-κB expression, and IFNG-response program was shared by melanoma Degradation-High and ccRCC measured Sarcosine-Low tumors and was fully replicated by exact-set GSEA in TCGA NSCLC Degradation-High tumors.

Avoid:

- “Sarcosine activates TNFR2.”
- “NFKB2/RELB activation was proven in both cohorts.”
- “The pathway occurs within a specific immune cell.”
- “The arrows establish a causal sequence.”
